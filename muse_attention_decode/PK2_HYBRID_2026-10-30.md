## Next controlled mechanism: remove range conversions, keep the same values

The scalar [official FA4 helper](https://github.com/Dao-AILab/flash-attention/blob/4616f5116d33e3ba3cbb71d582caffcfcb49649c/flash_attn/cute/utils.py)
uses directed-rounding add-bias range reduction and mantissa-bit exponent
reconstruction. Source helpers/copyright/BSD3license/AUTHORS are pinned in
`research/public/flash_attention_4616f51/`. Its packedFP32x2 variant is not
ported to H200. The scalar operations are supported on sm90a.

Our standalone `exp_range_compiler_probe.cu`,compiled using Compiler Explorer
NVCC13.0/sm90a/-O3,shows the repaired floor path emits FRND.FLOOR,F2I.FLOOR
and FADD. The bias version emits FADD.RM,FADD,FADD and no FRND/F2I in that
region. Both use10registers/zero stack/spill bytes. Device SASS was inspected;
host assembly was not mistaken for GPU output. Full request/response in
`EXP_RANGE_COMPILER_PROBE_2026-10-30.json`.

`tools/check_attention_exp_range_equivalence.py` uses exact rational sums to
verify directed FP32 rounding. All66,780 grid/boundary/subnormal cases have
bit-identical polynomial outputs for the two range reductions. Coefficients,
lower clamp and overflow guard stay fixed. This is sampled CPU equivalence,
not a full-kernel GPU proof. The [NVIDIA throughput table](https://docs.nvidia.com/cuda/archive/12.9.1/cuda-c-programming-guide/index.html#arithmetic-instructions)
motivates distinguishing arithmetic/conversion classes; it does not prove
that conversion and MUFU contend on the same physical unit in our kernel.

This supplies a distinct mechanism for one follow-up, not a blind polynomial
degree/stage sweep. Prefill's existing polynomial already uses a centered
add-bias trick: its earlier negative results are not explained by this finding
and that closed family is not reopened.
